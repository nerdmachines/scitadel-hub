# Contributing

scitadel-hub currently has a single author, and **external pull requests are not accepted yet**. The dual licence (AGPL-3.0-only OR commercial) needs a clear copyright line.

Issues and bug reports are welcome: open them here, or report through the scitadel client.

When outside contributions open up, every contributor will sign an Apache-style Individual Contributor License Agreement first. A DCO sign-off alone can't carry the commercial half of the dual licence.
